Bunnies

Bunnies are very cute and most are very fluffy. They come in all kinds of colors like brown, white, black, etc. They also have very big ears. Bunnies are strictly herbivores and usually eat grass. They are common pets.

Fennec foxes

Fennec foxes are pretty small animals. They also have huge ears like bunnies do. They are usually sand colored, but can be other natural colors as well. They mostly gather plants, but they also eat rodents and smaller animals. They can also be kept as pets like bunnies can.
